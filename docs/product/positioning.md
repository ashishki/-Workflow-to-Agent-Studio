# Positioning

## Category

Pre-implementation AI roadmap studio for SMB operations and AI automation
consultants.

## Market Contrast

| Alternative | What It Does | Gap |
|-------------|--------------|-----|
| Generic ChatGPT prompt | Produces advice quickly | Usually lacks evidence links, privacy gates, cost assumptions, and review trail |
| AI agent builder | Builds runtime automation | Starts too late, after the workflow and risk decisions should already be made |
| Consulting template | Creates a polished PDF | Often not reproducible, versioned, or validator-backed |
| Process-mining suite | Finds process patterns from system logs | Heavyweight and connector-dependent for SMB discovery |
| RPA/no-code platform | Automates known steps | Does not decide what should be AI, script, RPA, or manual |

## Differentiation

Workflow-to-Agent Studio should win by being:

- evidence-linked;
- local-first by default;
- privacy-aware;
- deterministic where safety matters;
- honest about assumptions and uncertainty;
- useful before implementation starts;
- exportable as an implementation handoff.

## Messaging By Segment

### Small Businesses

"Know where AI will actually pay off before you buy a bot or hire an agency."

Emphasis:

- quick wins;
- low-risk automation;
- budget ranges;
- no raw customer-data upload requirement.

### Mid-Market Operations

"Prioritize AI initiatives by impact, data readiness, privacy risk, and delivery
complexity."

Emphasis:

- staged rollout;
- governance;
- owner assignment;
- internal approval artifacts.

### AI Consultants And Agencies

"Turn discovery calls and SOPs into evidence-linked AI roadmaps in hours, not
days."

Emphasis:

- repeatable methodology;
- client-ready deliverables;
- assumptions register;
- pattern library and implementation handoff.

### Internal Automation Teams

"Use one intake and scoring layer before the team starts building AI
solutions."

Emphasis:

- backlog shaping;
- risk controls;
- evaluation criteria;
- do-not-automate decisions.

## Non-Claim Language

Avoid:

- "guaranteed ROI";
- "fully compliant";
- "replace your staff";
- "automatically builds the agent";
- "safe to send all customer data";
- "legal/medical/financial advice automation";
- "no human needed".

Prefer:

- "implementation-ready roadmap";
- "evidence-linked recommendation";
- "assumption-backed estimate";
- "human-reviewed handoff";
- "privacy mode recommendation";
- "bounded pilot".
