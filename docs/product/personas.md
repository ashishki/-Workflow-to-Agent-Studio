# Personas

## AI Automation Consultant

Goal: turn messy discovery material into a credible implementation roadmap for a
client.

Needs:

- fast workflow decomposition;
- evidence-linked recommendations;
- clear client-facing language;
- cost and team assumptions;
- exportable Markdown/PDF;
- do-not-automate rationale.

Success:

- can show a client a useful roadmap after one discovery session;
- can hand scoped tasks to implementation engineers;
- can defend why a recommendation is safe or unsafe.

## SMB Owner / Operator

Goal: decide where to invest in AI without overbuying a generic bot.

Needs:

- plain-language priorities;
- quick wins;
- budget range;
- privacy explanation;
- implementation order;
- risks and human review requirements.

Success:

- knows what to do in the next 30, 60, and 90 days;
- avoids unsafe automation;
- gets a credible quote or implementation scope.

## COO / Head Of Operations

Goal: create a controlled AI automation backlog.

Needs:

- process inventory;
- priority scoring;
- privacy/security bands;
- owner assignment;
- rollout gates;
- evidence and audit trail.

Success:

- initiatives are prioritized consistently;
- risky work is delayed or routed to review;
- implementation teams receive scoped handoffs.

## Internal Automation Engineer

Goal: avoid building the wrong thing.

Needs:

- workflow steps and systems;
- deterministic vs LLM-owned boundaries;
- integration assumptions;
- evaluation cases;
- failure modes;
- acceptance criteria.

Success:

- receives a buildable task plan;
- understands what must stay human-approved;
- can write tests and evals from the roadmap.
